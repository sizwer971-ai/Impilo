01 ABOUT THE CLUB

More than a game.
A way forward.

Impilo 0720 Tennis Club was formed in 2019 and registered in July 2020. Based in Mamelodi, City of Tshwane, we are a community-based tennis development organisation rooted in the Black community of Mamelodi. We believe sport can help young people build confidence, discipline, healthy habits and new opportunities.

Our aim is to make tennis more accessible by connecting players, families, schools and the wider community through participation and development.

The club is affiliated with Tennis South Africa, Gauteng Tennis Association and Tshwane Tennis Federation Development.

04 ROOTED IN MAMELODI

A local game.
A shared future.

Based in Mamelodi, Impilo 0720 Tennis Club is part of a community surrounded by six schools and connected to tennis activities and facilities at HM Pitje Stadium.

The club collaborates with surrounding schools through an annual schools programme that includes a schools league and school tournaments.
